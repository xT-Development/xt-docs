---
title: "Features"
---

* Aim an allowed weapon at an ambient NPC to hold them up
* Smooth, blended surrender animation — the ped turns to face you, raises their hands, then eases down into a kneel
* Random reaction on every hold up — the ped surrenders, flees, or fights back
  * If the ped fights, a random chance they pull a weapon chosen from the config
* Configurable chance each robbery calls the police, wired to your own dispatch resource
* Rob the ped through ox_target, qb-target, or sleepless_interact
* Server-authoritative payouts — the server re-checks the ped, distance, job, cop count, and cooldowns before it pays anyone
* Per-player and per-ped cooldowns stop callback spam and double-robbing
* A `robbed` statebag is set once a ped is robbed, flees, or fights, so it can't be robbed again
* Required cop count, or zero
* Configurable cash payout and item loot, each with its own chance
* Blacklisted jobs that can't rob locals
* Configurable blocked ped types — cops, medics, firemen, swat, and army are off limits by default
* Multi-framework via [Renewed-Lib](https://github.com/Renewed-Scripts/Renewed-Lib) — QB, QBX, OX, ESX, ND
* Open config functions for money, items, and police dispatch — use any inventory or dispatch resource
