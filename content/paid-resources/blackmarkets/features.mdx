title: "Features"
---

### Live Location Management

* Admins and developers can manage locations while live
* Add/create new locations
* Remove black market locations
* Edit black market info (item set, ped model, ped scenario)

### Configuration

* Set max amount of black markets that will spawn each restart
* Random markets are chosen to spawn each restart
* Each market can sell different items
* Create item sets to choose from when creating blackmarkets
