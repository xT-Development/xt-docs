**Purchase:** [https://store.xthrasherrr.dev/resources/6489649](https://store.xthrasherrr.dev/resources/6489649)

Players gather at an event ped, create or join a lobby, and are dropped into a blacked-out map in
their own routing bucket. A few of them are secretly **Slashers** - masked, armed, and blind and
invisible while the rest hide. Survivors hear their own heartbeat as a slasher closes in. The last survivor
is armed before time runs out, and anyone eliminated lingers on the map as a ghost and spectates
the hunt.

Every map and every event ped is built in-game with `/slasherbuilder`, on a live server. Changes
reach every player immediately - no restart, no file editing.
