## The game

* **Hide, then hunt.** Slashers start masked, frozen and invisible, with their screen blacked out,
  while survivors scatter. When the hiding time is up, their screen clears and the hunt begins.
* **Heartbeat.** A survivor hears their own heartbeat when a slasher is close. The distance is
  measured on the server, so a modified client cannot use it to find the slashers.
* **The last survivor fights back.** Shortly before a round ends, the last survivor standing is
  armed with the lobby's weapon.
* **Rounds and score.** Slashers win a round by eliminating every survivor. Survivors win by
  eliminating every slasher, or by lasting until the timer runs out. Roles are re-picked each round,
  and the game goes to whoever wins the most rounds - or a draw.
* **Ghosts and spectating.** An eliminated player's ghost stays on the map where they fell, and
  they spectate the surviving slashers until the round ends.
* **Blackout.** Every round is played at midnight with the city lights off, and the weather and
  clock are handed back when the game ends.
* **Out of bounds.** Leaving the map starts a countdown. Players who do not return in time are
  eliminated.
* **Native GTA Online sounds** for the heartbeat, round start and end, win and loss.
* **Slasher outfits**, set separately for male and female peds.

## Lobbies

* **A full lobby browser** - create, edit, delete, join, leave and join a random lobby, with a live
  player list. No context menus.
* **Password-locked lobbies.** Passwords are checked on the server and never sent to a client.
* **Lobby settings** the host picks: name, password, map, weapon, max players, max slashers,
  rounds, round length and hiding time. Every setting is bounded by limits you set in the config.
* **Routing buckets.** Each game runs in its own bucket, so players in a game cannot be seen or
  touched by anyone outside it.

## In-game HUD

* Round, countdown, survivors alive, score and the current phase. Passive - it never takes your
  mouse.
* **Scoreboard** - hold `TAB` for the full board. A living slasher's identity is hidden from
  living survivors; dead players and slashers see everything.

## Map builder

* **Build maps in the world** with a freecam: click to place the boundary and the spawn points,
  hold a key and scroll to set the zone's height as you go, undo and redo.
* **Edit, clone, enable, disable or delete** any map. A disabled map disappears from the
  create-lobby list without being deleted.
* **Place event peds** - the NPCs players walk up to - with their model, scenario, heading and map
  blip.
* **Live.** A saved map or ped appears for every player at once.
* **Several admins at once.** Each map and ped is locked to whoever is editing it.

See [Map Builder](/paid-resources/slasher-minigame/map-builder).

## Server-authoritative

* The slasher list is never sent to living survivors.
* Deaths are verified against the server's own view of the player's health.
* Every lobby setting and every map is checked against your configured limits on the server, so a
  modified client cannot start a 999-round game or save a 50,000-point map.
* The builder's permission is checked on the server on every action, not just when the menu opens.
